Welcome to VUT CourseCorrect

Your academic success partner at Vaal University of Technology. We provide comprehensive tutoring for the courses listed on our site, supporting students from first year through to third year.

Book a Tutor Today

All Faculties Covered

From Applied and Computer Sciences to Engineering, Humanities, and Management Sciences - we have tutors for every faculty at VUT.

Year 1 to Year 3

Whether you are a first-year student adjusting to university life or a third-year student tackling advanced modules, our tutors are here to help.

Flexible Scheduling

Book sessions that fit your timetable. We offer morning, afternoon, and evening time slots to accommodate your schedule.

How It Works

  1. Find your course on the Courses page.
  2. Fill in the booking form with your details, course code, and preferred time.
  3. Get confirmation - our team contacts you within 24 hours to confirm your tutor.
  4. Attend your session and improve your understanding.

Why Students Choose Us

  • One-on-one and group sessions
  • Exam review and assignment help
  • Tutors matched to your specific course
  • Simple online booking, 24-hour confirmation
