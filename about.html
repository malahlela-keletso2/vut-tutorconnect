About VUT CourseCorrect

We help Vaal University of Technology students understand their courses, prepare for assessments, and build the confidence to succeed.

Who We Are

VUT CourseCorrect is a student tutoring service built for the Vaal University of Technology community. It was started in 2026 to make academic support easy to find and easy to book.

We cover courses across four faculties and three year levels, so first-year students and final-year students can both find help in one place.

Our Mission and Values

  • Mission: To empower every VUT student with clear, practical academic support.
  • Accessible: Booking is online, simple, and open to every faculty.
  • Focused: Each tutor is matched to your specific course and needs.
  • Supportive: We explain, encourage, and help you build your own understanding.

What We Offer

  • Tutoring for courses in four faculties
  • Sessions for 1st, 2nd and 3rd year students
  • One-on-one and small group sessions
  • Exam review and assignment help
  • Morning, afternoon and evening time slots

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tutor?
Use the Book Tutor page, complete the form, and submit it.
When will I hear back?
A member of our team contacts you within 24 hours to confirm your tutor and final session time.
Which courses are covered?
See the Courses page for every course by faculty and year level.
Can I change or cancel my booking?
Yes. Reply to your acknowledgement email or write to coursecorrect40@gmail.com.
